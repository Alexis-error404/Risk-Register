# Risk Treatment Plan

## R-001 — Privileged Account Compromise
**Inherent:** 20 Critical  
**Actions:** MFA, dedicated admin accounts, privileged-group reviews, abnormal-authentication monitoring, break-glass governance.  
**Target residual:** 10 High.

## R-003 — Critical Vulnerabilities
**Inherent:** 20 Critical  
**Actions:** Severity/exposure-based remediation SLAs, asset tracking, priority for internet-facing/actively exploited vulnerabilities, exception/compensating-control process, validation.  
**Target residual:** 8 Moderate.

## R-006 — Phishing
**Inherent:** 20 Critical  
**Actions:** MFA, email controls, awareness, simulations, reporting mechanism, conditional access where appropriate, compromised-account playbook.  
**Target residual:** 10 High.

## R-005 — Third-Party Risk
**Actions:** Vendor criticality tiers, due diligence, appropriate assurance evidence, contractual security requirements, material finding tracking, breach notification requirements, periodic reassessment.

## Before Closing Remediation
Ask: What changed? Who owns it? What proves completion? Who validates effectiveness? What residual risk remains? Does remaining risk require formal acceptance?
