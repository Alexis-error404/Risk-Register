# Executive Cyber Risk Summary

## Overall Posture
The simulated organization has several Critical inherent risks concentrated in identity, vulnerability management, detection, phishing, third parties, and resilience.

## Priorities
1. **Identity:** prioritize MFA and privileged-access governance.
2. **Vulnerabilities:** establish remediation timelines, ownership, exceptions, and validation.
3. **Detection:** formalize log monitoring and escalation.
4. **Human risk:** combine awareness with strong authentication and reporting.
5. **Third parties:** tier vendors, conduct due diligence, establish contractual requirements, and reassess critical relationships.

## Leadership Decisions
- Approve privileged MFA priority.
- Establish vulnerability remediation SLAs.
- Assign owners for Critical/High risks.
- Define formal risk-acceptance authority.
- Require periodic critical-vendor reassessment.
- Review cyber risk quarterly.

## Metrics
Critical/High risk count · overdue actions · risk aging · MFA coverage · vulnerability remediation time · privileged access reviews · critical vendors assessed · recovery-test success · tabletop completion.
