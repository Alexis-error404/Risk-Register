# Risk Assessment Methodology

## Likelihood
1 Rare · 2 Unlikely · 3 Possible · 4 Likely · 5 Almost Certain

## Impact
1 Minimal · 2 Minor · 3 Moderate · 4 Major · 5 Severe

## Simplified Portfolio Calculation
**Likelihood × Impact = Inherent Risk**

1–4 Low · 5–9 Moderate · 10–14 High · 15–25 Critical

## Process
1. Identify assets, threats, vulnerabilities, dependencies, control gaps, and third-party exposures.
2. Write cause-event-impact risk statements.
3. Assess likelihood.
4. Assess impact across confidentiality, integrity, availability, financial, regulatory, operational, and reputation factors.
5. Determine inherent risk.
6. Evaluate existing controls.
7. Select treatment: **Mitigate, Accept, Transfer, or Avoid**.
8. Reassess expected residual risk.
9. Assign a risk owner and remediation/action owners.
10. Monitor and reassess.

> Residual risk should not be calculated by blindly subtracting a control score. It should be reassessed using the organization's approved methodology and expected/validated control effectiveness.
