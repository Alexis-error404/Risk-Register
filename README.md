# Cybersecurity Risk Register Portfolio

A simulated enterprise-style cybersecurity risk register demonstrating **GRC, cyber risk analysis, NIST control mapping, treatment planning, remediation tracking, and executive communication**.

> **Portfolio disclaimer:** The organization, risks, ratings, owners, and decisions are fictional and created for educational/interview purposes.

## Scenario
A fictional organization uses Active Directory, cloud services, remote access, endpoints, third-party vendors, and centralized IT services. I am acting as a Junior GRC / Cyber Risk Analyst responsible for identifying, analyzing, prioritizing, and tracking cyber risk.

## Method
**Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**, using 1–5 scales.

| Score | Rating |
|---:|---|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Moderate |
| 10–14 | High |
| 15–25 | Critical |
